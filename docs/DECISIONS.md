| D34 | 2026-10-19 | **Grade-level monotone recalibration** of the champion (`calibration.grade_recalibration`). One anchor per master-scale grade, from train out-of-fold PDs (mean predicted log-odds → observed log-odds with a 0.5 prior; grades with n < 500 get no anchor). Anchors are made non-decreasing by isotonic regression, then strictly increasing, and PDs are mapped by linear interpolation in log-odds (slope 1 beyond the ends). The map is strictly increasing, so rank order, Gini and SHAP reason codes are unchanged. It falls back to identity when fewer than two grades qualify. ECL uses the calibrated PD, while the relative SICR test compares the *uncalibrated* champion with the (uncalibrated) origination proxy, like with like. | Fixes the D33 calibration findings. On OOT (never seen by the fit): ECE 0.0035 → 0.0020, slope 0.957 → 1.004, HL p 0.006 → 0.050, red grades 2 → 0, Gini 0.5602 unchanged. Applying calibrated PDs to staging would have inflated Stage 2 (3,572 → 3,919) through a calibration artefact, hence the uncalibrated staging input. ECL coverage 4.52% → 4.59%. The one decile rank-order break remains: it's a ranking property that a monotone recalibration can't change. |
