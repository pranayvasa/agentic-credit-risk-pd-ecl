| D20 | 2026-10-18 | The challenger LightGBM uses all 112 candidate features (raw values) with fixed config hyperparameters. | D21 | 2026-10-18 | The scorecard is capped at 20 features before backward elimination (wrong sign, then p > 0.05). | D22 | 2026-10-18 | Hard limits set by the author: at most **15 scorecard variables**, and LightGBM `max_depth` between 1 and 10 (no unlimited depth), both validated in the config schema. Tuned by `acr tune` (3-fold CV inside train only, 1-SE rule; reports in `outputs/tuning/`) and frozen: scorecard cap 15 with 6 bins (13 variables kept); LightGBM depth 5, 15 leaves, min_child_samples 1000, λ 10. | Keeps the model lean. Scorecard CV Gini at cap 15 was 0.496, against 0.469–0.480 at 8–12 variables, with no stability gain from fewer variables (overfit gap about 0.01 at every size). LightGBM depth 5 matched the best CV Gini (0.568) and cut the overfit gap from 0.21 to 0.13. Full run: the challenger's train-to-OOT Gini gap fell from 0.159 to 0.103, and champion OOT Gini moved from 0.507 to 0.501 with four fewer variables. |
| D23 | 2026-10-18 | The validator is read-only. It imports no fitting code and verifies every model artefact's SHA-256 against the model node's checksums, both before and after it runs; a mismatch is a red finding that halts an enforced run. Findings come from deterministic rules with thresholds in `config/gates.yaml` (severity info → red). Only red findings drive the gate: halt on full runs, warn on `--sample`. | Mirrors second-line independence and makes it testable. Tests tamper with an artefact and confirm the halt, and confirm by source inspection that the validator has no fitting code. |
| D24 | 2026-10-18 | Grade calibration uses a one-sided binomial test on under-prediction (red at p ≤ 0.01, amber at p ≤ 0.05). HL is reported with a sample-size caveat. Stress PSI is a ladder (under-30s up-weighted ×3 / ×10 / ×50 in the OOT book), not a single point. | Under-prediction is the prudentially relevant direction. On the full run the ladder gives 0.021 / 0.132 / 0.339 (green / amber / red), which shows that score PSI only detects large mix shifts, so monitoring adds per-feature CSI. |
| D25 | 2026-10-19 | The current split stays. `application_test.csv` has no TARGET, so it cannot be a validation partition. It is loaded as an **external, unlabelled** population: legs build its features, the model scores it (never fits on it), the validator runs score-PSI and CSI against train, and full runs export Kaggle submission files (`outputs/<run>/kaggle/`). Every Gini is reported with its AUC. | The author proposed using application_test as OOT, but without outcomes it cannot support discrimination or calibration testing, and Kaggle drew it from the same period, so it is not temporal either. The comparison with public kernels was AUC (about 0.79) against our Gini; the challenger's OOT AUC is 0.788. Full run: external score PSI 0.0018; EXT_SOURCE_1 CSI 0.085 on the external population. |
| D26 | 2026-10-19 | Term and EIR: AMT_CREDIT and AMT_ANNUITY cannot identify both, so the monthly rate per contract type is the median rate implied by approved prior Home Credit loans of **train customers** (credit, annuity and CNT_PAYMENT are all known there). The term is solved from the annuity formula at that rate, and the same rate is the EIR for discounting. Revolving loans (no implied rate available) use the 2% monthly fallback, a 24-month behavioural life (para 46(2)(iii)) and CCF 1.0. | Data-driven rather than invented. Full run: 3.33% a month for cash loans (about 48% a year, consistent with high-cost consumer lending); median term 33 months. A loan-level EIR is not identifiable from this data; the rate is segment-level. |
| D27 | 2026-10-19 | ECL design: the engine computes unit ECL (LGD = 1), so the LGD sensitivity is exact. Staging is fixed across scenarios, and PD scalars apply to the 12-month PD before the term structure. Constant hazard is the primary lifetime method; the Markov method keeps the same 12-month PD and only reshapes later years. Final ECL = max(model ECL, RBI floor × EAD₀), and both are reported. QC checks: scenario weights sum to 1, ECL is non-negative, coverage and realised default rate rise with stage, and lifetime PD ≥ 12-month PD for Stage 2 loans of 12+ months. | Makes every assumption separable and testable. Full run on the 76,878-account OOT book: Stage 1 / 2 / 3 = 96.8% / 3.1% / 0.1%; realised default rate 7.9% / 13.0% / 23.9%; coverage after floors 4.1% / 14.3% / 70.0% (total 4.48%); floors add 0.9% to ECL; Markov ECL is 5.7% below constant hazard. |
